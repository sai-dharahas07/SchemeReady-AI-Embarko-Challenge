# Embarko 2026 competition scorecard and execution plan

Source of truth: https://embarko.ai/challenge/rules and https://embarko.ai/challenge. Re-check both pages before entry because the sponsor can update the rules and deadlines.

## Official judging structure

| Component | Official maximum | What judges assess |
|---|---:|---|
| Work (app) | 400 | Appearance, originality, phone usability |
| Video | 400 | Opening hook, clarity, storytelling, showing Embarko |
| Reach | 200 | Views and engagement visible on submitted public posts |

The rules also describe 50 points for an eligible submission and 50 points for each submitted post link, up to three. Referral and LinkedIn bonus mechanisms are described separately in the official rules. No bots, bought views, paid promotion or artificial engagement.

## Product priorities mapped to Work (400)

1. **Visual finish:** consistent SchemeReady AI identity, high-quality typography, calm public-service palette, responsive layouts, polished empty/loading/error states.
2. **Originality:** make Proof Gap Finder and preparation readiness the signature, rather than presenting another generic scheme search page.
3. **Phone usability:** test the full citizen journey on a real phone; keep tap targets, forms, cards and navigation comfortable at narrow widths.
4. **Trust:** distinguish verified records from demo/unverified records; never invent eligibility, amounts, deadlines, integrations or approval probability.
5. **One memorable action:** from a potential match, show satisfied conditions, unmet conditions, missing information and a personalised next-step checklist.

## Recommended 75-second video storyboard

| Time | Scene | What to show |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sec | Hook | “What if finding a government benefit was only the first step?” Show a citizen staring at a confusing list of schemes. |
| 4–12 sec | Problem and idea | Introduce SchemeReady AI: discover, understand and prepare. |
| 12–22 sec | Onboarding | Enter a small, non-sensitive profile. Explain that gender is optional and income bands are treated carefully. |
| 22–35 sec | Explainable match | Open one potential scheme and show matching conditions, missing confirmations and source link. |
| 35–48 sec | Signature moment | Open the Proof Gap checklist/action plan and mark a document as ready. Show readiness changing only from checklist completion. |
| 48–57 sec | Application tracker | Change a self-managed application status; clarify the app does not submit or receive government status. |
| 57–69 sec | Embarko | Show the actual Embarko deployment flow and the live `yourname.embarko.app` URL. Do not fake this footage. |
| 69–75 sec | Close | Show the app on a phone, URL and `#DeployWithEmbarko`. |

The official video duration is 40 seconds to 3 minutes. Vertical framing is recommended on the challenge page.

## Distribution checklist

- Publish a public video on Instagram, X and YouTube if feasible, each with `#DeployWithEmbarko` and the live app link.
- Submit every public post URL with the entry.
- Keep the app online and all submitted posts public until the results as required.
- Use captions and readable on-screen text; add subtitles.
- Ask classmates and relevant student/community groups for genuine feedback and shares. Do not buy engagement or automate likes/views.
- Use the official referral link and LinkedIn bonus options only as allowed by the current rules.

## Eligibility and submission caution

The current rules state that entries are individual, not team entries, and one entry is allowed per person. Ensure the actual entrant owns and can demonstrate the submitted work. The main challenge page currently lists a 30 November 2026 registration deadline; the rules page says the closing date will be announced. Confirm the live registration/submission state on the official challenge page before relying on a date.
